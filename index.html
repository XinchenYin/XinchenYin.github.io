Research

My research focuses on scalable, learning-based methods for robot manipulation. I work across the full robotics stack, from hardware and data collection to policy learning, simulation, and real-world deployment. My long-term goals are (1) to enable robots to learn efficiently from Internet-scale data and (2) to equip robots with human-level manipulation capabilities.

Currently, I am interested in:

  • Leveraging foundation models for robotics
  • Learning from cross-embodiment data
  • World action models and world models for robot learning
